### Suggested Repo Structure

```
hackathon-v2-{platform}/
├── README.md
├── src/
│   ├── frontend/
│   └── backend/
├── database/
│   └── schema.sql (or migrations/)
├── docs/
│   ├── prompts/           ← AI prompt log
│   ├── hitl/              ← HITL intervention log
│   ├── architecture.md
│   └── assessment-report.md
└── tests/
```
